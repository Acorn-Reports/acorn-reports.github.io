Acorn — Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

What Acorn accesses

Acorn connects to a single Gmail account owned by its operator. It reads only messages containing report attachments sent to that inbox, and it may label messages and create drafts.

How data is used

Report attachments are loaded into a private dashboard for the operator's own use. Acorn does not send email on its own.

Storage

Data is stored privately on the operator's own computer. It is not stored on public servers.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Acorn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).
